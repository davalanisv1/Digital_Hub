## David's Digital Hub
Welcome to the repository for David's Digital Hub, a personal portfolio website built to showcase academic achievements, hands-on engineering projects, extracurricular activities, and media tracking.
This project serves as a live digital portfolio reflecting educational growth, technical skill development, and community involvement.
## Project Overview
This website is a customized implementation of the responsive Landed template by HTML5 UP. It is structured to provide clean, accessible navigation through multiple areas of personal and professional growth:

* Academics & School Work: Tracks formal high school transcripts, coursework details, and the transition from an International Baccalaureate (IB) system at Liceo de Apodaca in Mexico to Northville High School in Michigan, USA.
* Clubs & Extracurriculars: Documents participation in engineering and community groups, including FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC) Team 548 (Robostangs), the Northville High School Aerospace Club, and volunteer service at Maybury Farm.
* Certifications: Highlights technical, industry-aligned credentials pursued independently and through the Mechatronics program at the William D. Ford Technology & Career Center.
* Projects & Crafts: Displays a gallery of independent, hands-on DIY hobbies, woodworking, civil volunteer projects, and upcoming robotic builds.
* Media Tracking: Serves as a digital library cataloging all-time favorite books, movies, and automated weekly music listening metrics.

## Technical Specifications
The site is built with a focus on clean, responsive, and performance-oriented design utilizing foundational web technologies:

* HTML5: Semantic markup structuring the content across all hub pages.
* CSS3: Responsive layouts and custom styling modified from the base theme.
* JavaScript: Integrated modules for dynamic components, including live scrobble and playback tracking.
* Design Core: Adapted from the open-source Landed template by HTML5 UP.

## Repository Structure

* /home.html - The main landing page and introduction to the portfolio.
* /school.html - Comprehensive academic records, grade tracking, and transcript histories.
* /clubs.html - Documentation of extracurricular involvement and community service.
* /certs.html - Professional badges and industry certifications.
* /projects.html - Photo galleries and technical summaries of physical and digital creations.
* /tracking.html - Personal reviews and active API logs tracking book, film, and music consumption.
