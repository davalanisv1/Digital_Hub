Media Tracking

Books, films, and music that inspire and interest me

Books

Books offer knowledge, perspective, and an opportunity to step outside everyday routines. Depending on the story, reading can inspire creativity, encourage reflection, or provide a welcome change of pace.

Percy Jackson

This series combines ancient mythology with the modern world in a fast-paced adventure that remains one of my favorite reads.

La Tierra de las Historias

This series reimagines familiar fairy tales and brings their characters together in an imaginative story world.

Tokyo Ghoul

This dark series explores identity and conflict through a tense story that kept me engaged throughout.

Blue Lock

This high-stakes sports manga follows ambitious players competing to become Japan's top striker.

Straight 'Til Morning

This entry in the Twisted Tales series offers an alternative take on a familiar story.

The Glass Menagerie

Tennessee Williams's play examines memory, family relationships, and the illusions people use to cope with difficult circumstances.

Macbeth

Shakespeare's tragedy explores ambition, prophecy, and the consequences of a pursuit of power.

Romeo and Juliet

Shakespeare's tragedy follows two young lovers caught between family conflict and their own decisions.

The Metamorphosis

Kafka's story uses an extraordinary transformation to explore isolation, family, and social expectations.

In the Penal Colony

Kafka's story considers justice, authority, and punishment through the workings of a disturbing penal system.

The Judgement

Kafka's story explores family tension, guilt, and psychological conflict.

Go to the Distance

This Twisted Tale reimagines a familiar heroic journey and its outcome.

Night

Elie Wiesel's memoir recounts his experience during the Holocaust and reflects on survival and memory.

Twisted Tale Anthology

This collection presents alternate versions of familiar stories from a range of perspectives.

The Cask of Amontillado

Poe's short story builds psychological suspense around revenge and a carefully planned crime.

Self-Reliance

Emerson's essay argues for individual integrity, self-trust, and independent thought.

The Catcher in the Rye

Salinger's novel follows a teenager wrestling with alienation, identity, and the transition to adulthood.

The Paper Menagerie

This award-winning short story combines magical realism with themes of family, language, and cultural heritage.

It Won't Always be Like This

This graphic memoir reflects on family relationships, change, and personal growth.

Things Fall Apart

Achebe's novel portrays life in pre-colonial Nigeria and the effects of British colonialism.

Electronics from the Ground Up

This guide introduces electronic components, circuits, and practical hardware projects.

Handbook of Model Rocketry

This handbook covers model rocket design, construction, and safe flight practices.

High-Powered Rocketry

This guide addresses the design, construction, and safe launch of high-power rockets.

Movies

Cinema brings together visual art, music, and performance to tell stories and explore complex ideas. I especially enjoy films that imagine unfamiliar worlds or invite questions about science and reality.

Interstellar

Interstellar is my favorite film. Its cinematography, score, characters, and exploration of space make it an experience I return to often.

Oppenheimer

Oppenheimer is among my favorite recent films. I especially appreciate its score, cinematography, and performances, and it ranks near the top of my all-time list.

The Martian

The Martian is an engaging science-fiction film. While its cinematography and score are not its main strengths for me, the resourceful protagonist and compelling plot make it well worth watching.

Project Hail Mary

Project Hail Mary is one of my favorite films, alongside Interstellar. I enjoy its score, cinematography, and performances, and have watched it many times.

A Dog's Purpose

This is one of the few films that has made me cry, and its emotional story has stayed with me.

Everest

Everest is the first film I remember watching. I recall watching it with my family one evening and being moved by it, though I remember little of the plot now. That early memory has stayed with me.

Coraline

Coraline is a family favorite that my sister and I both enjoy.

Music

Music is part of my daily routine. It helps me focus while working, shift my mood, and reconnect with meaningful memories.

Boleros

I enjoy romantic guitar music, especially Julio Jaramillo, known as "El Ruiseñor de América," and groups such as Los Panchos, Los Tres Caballeros, Los Tres Ases, and Los Tecolines.

Pasillos

Pasillos are traditional songs known for their expressive melodies and lyrics. I often listen to Julio Jaramillo, Olimpo Cárdenas, and Silva y Villalba.

Baladas

I enjoy the expressive vocals of José José, Juan Gabriel, and Mon Laferte.

Pop & Indie Pop

This collection spans contemporary pop and indie music, including artists such as Milo J, Mon Laferte, Nasa Histoires, Will Wood, Malcolm Todd, and Jósean Log.

Rock & Alternative

My rock playlists range from Rock en Español to grunge, with artists such as Los Enanitos Verdes, Hombres G, El Cuarteto de Nos, Caifanes, Trueno, Radiohead, and Nirvana.

Jazz & Vocal Standards

This collection pairs vocal standards by Frank Sinatra and Paul Anka with contemporary artists such as Laufey, Kevin Kaarl, Frank Sark, and Zak Azoury.
