Books

One of the most essential sources for a human are books, they provide knowledge, an escape from reality, inspiration, creativity or reflection depending on the story, it gives us a breath, a break from our daily lives.

Percy Jackson

An incredible series that blends ancient mythology with the modern world, making for an unforgettable and fast-paced adventure.

La Tierra de las Historias

A mesmerizing journey through a fairy tale world where classic stories come alive in brilliant, unexpected ways.

Tokyo Ghoul

A dark, thrilling dive into a world of conflict and hidden identity that keeps you completely glued to every chapter.

Blue Lock

An intense, high-stakes sports manga focused on peak egoism, striking skill, and the ultimate quest to become the best striker.

Straight 'Til Morning

A fascinating Twisted Tale that flips a well-known narrative completely on its head with brilliant storytelling hooks.

The Glass Menagerie

A deeply moving play that explores memory, family fragility, and the delicate illusions we build to protect ourselves.

Macbeth

Shakespeare's intense tragic masterpiece full of unchecked ambition, dark prophecies, and a gripping downfall.

Romeo and Juliet

The definitive classic tale of star-crossed lovers, ancient family feuds, and the heavy price of impulsive passion.

The Metamorphosis

Kafka's surreal and thought-provoking classic about isolation, absurd transformation, and the burdens of expectation.

In the Penal Colony

A gripping and chilling Kafka story exploring complex themes of justice, mechanical punishment, and structural devotion.

The Judgement

A powerful psychological narrative focused on strained family dynamics, sudden guilt, and inner conflict.

Go to the Distance

Another brilliant entry in the Twisted Tale collection that reimagines heroics and fates with sharp emotional resonance.

Night

Elie Wiesel's deeply profound, haunting, and unforgettable first-hand account of survival and memory.

Twisted Tale Anthology

A wonderful collection of reimagined classics that brings distinct perspectives to familiar journeys.

The Cask of Amontillado

Poe's masterpiece of chilling dark revenge, perfect execution, and psychological suspense down in the catacombs.

Self-Reliance

Ralph Waldo Emerson's foundational essay highlighting individual integrity, personal trust, and independent thinking.

The Catcher in the Rye

A classic exploration of teenage alienation, identity struggles, and navigating the complexities of growing up.

The Paper Menagerie

A beautifully poignant, award-winning short story combining magical realism with deep emotional themes of family and heritage.

It Won't Always be Like This

A poignant graphic memoir exploring family dynamics, dynamic changes, and personal growth.

Things Fall Apart

Chinua Achebe's masterpiece detailing pre-colonial life in Nigeria and the impact of European invasion.

Electronics from the Ground Up

A practical guide detailing practical electronic components, circuits, and hands-on hardware design.

Handbook of Model Rocketry

The essential official guide covering design, construction, and safe flight operations for model rockets.

High-Powered Rocketry

An advanced guide to building, launching, and certifying high-power motor rocket systems safely.

Movies

Cinema is a powerful extension of storytelling, blending visual artistry, music, and performance into an immersive experience. Movies do more than just entertain; they visually project complex ideas, transport us to distant galaxies, and make us question the limits of reality and science.

Interstellar

The. Best. Movie. Ever. To me there's no comparison between Interstellar and any other movie, it's one of a kind, the cinematic shots, the music, the characters, everything's perfect. It's irreplaceable.

Oppenheimer

Oh Man, it's one of the best movies of 2025, genuinely awesome, the music, the shots, everything, so peak, it would be between my second and third place of all times.

The Martian

Another goated movie, it's interesting, keeps you hooked the whole time, it's not the best regarding cinematography nor the most musically excelent movie, but the plot itself and the intelligence of the protagonist make it a movie worth watching.

Project Hail Mary

If I had to choose, this would be the second place, the music is awesome, the shots are awesome, the actors are awesome, I think it's at par with Interstellar, 10/10 movie, have watched it ten times already, will and would watch it ten more.

A Dog's Purpose

One of the two movies that genuinely made me cry, it's so peak.

Everest

This is the first movie I remember to have watched, I remember being in the sofa with my family watching TV, in the night, the first movie that made me cry, I can't even remember most of it to be honest, and I certainly can't remember why it made me cry, but it's always on the back of my mind.

Coraline

My sister loves it, I love it, my family loves it, what can I say.

Music

Music is a huge part of my daily routine. It helps me change my mood, reminds me of great memories, and helps me focus whenever I am working on projects.

Boleros

I love listening to classic romantic guitar music. My favorites are Julio Jaramillo—"El Ruiseñor de América"—and famous trios like Los Panchos, Los Tres Caballeros, Los Tres Ases, and Los Tecolines.

Pasillos

This is traditional, emotional music with deep meaning. I mostly listen to great artists like Jota Jota (Julio Jaramillo, J.J.), Olimpo Cárdenas, and Silva y Villalba.

Baladas

I enjoy classic, dramatic vocals. This section features José José—the GOAT—along with Juan Gabriel and Mon Laferte.

Pop & Indie Pop

A mix of modern songs and catchy indie music. Here I track artists like Milo J, Mon Laferte, Nasa Histoires, Will Wood, Malcolm Todd, and Jósean Log.

Rock & Alternative

My rock playlist goes from classic Rock en Español to energetic grunge. I frequently listen to Los Enanitos Verdes, Los Hombres G, El Cuarteto de Nos, Radiohead, and Nirvana.

Jazz & Vocal Standards

Smooth, relaxing music that is great for late nights. This features old-school singers like Frank Sinatra and Paul Anka, alongside modern artists like Laufey, Kevin Kaarl, Frank Sark, and Zak Azoury.
