Who Am I?

A small introduction

My name is David Alberto Alanís Alejandro, I was born in Mexico on February 2nd 2010, and I've had many ambitions, I've wanted to be a priest, a painter, an astronaut, a soldier, a teacher, a police man... but for the last two years I've wanted to be an engineer, an Electrical Engineer.

I moved to the United States on March 15th of 2025, and since I've tried to make a name for myself, I've done PLTW Engineering classes offered at my High School,joined Robotics Team, volunteered, joined Career Centers and, in general, tried my best.
