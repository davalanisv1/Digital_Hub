Robostangs - FRC Team 548

After my first semester at Northville High School, I recognized that extracurricular involvement offered valuable opportunities outside the classroom. Because of my interests in engineering and robotics, I joined Team 548, the Robostangs. During the initial months, I participated in the mechanical subteam, received hands-on training with shop equipment, and gained practical experience in manufacturing and assembly. I also developed stronger connections with peers who shared similar technical interests.

As the season progressed, however, the team dynamic shifted. A significant portion of the key project work was concentrated among a smaller group of senior members, and my responsibilities became limited to more basic support tasks. By the end of the season, I felt that the experience no longer aligned with the level of engagement and growth I was seeking. I chose to step away and redirect my attention to school and other opportunities that better supported my long-term goals. I participated in FRC robotics from October 2025 through June 2026.

Maybury Farm - Volunteering

After my sophomore year, I became more intentional about taking on responsibilities that reflected commitment and service. I volunteered at Maybury Farm, where I contributed to maintenance work while also supporting the barn and garden areas as needed. I committed four days a week for three to five hours each day, and although the opportunity began late in the summer, it gave me meaningful firsthand experience with hands-on community work. I was only able to participate for a brief period before school resumed, but I hope to return during the summers of 2027 and 2028.

NHS Aerospace Club

After earning my Junior Level 1 certification through the National Association of Rocketry, I joined Northville High School’s Aerospace Club to deepen my involvement in rocketry. I am working with the team to define a research- and design-focused role spanning areas such as payload development, component selection, assembly methods, motor research, simulations, and integration. I am especially interested in design work during the early planning and integration phases, while remaining available to support construction and guide newer members. I will also help maintain the club’s website at nhsaerospace.github.io. This experience is strengthening my skills in aerospace research, collaborative design, and technical communication.
